InkDynamics v4.0 — Ink That Mixes: Transparency, Overprint & Intersection Micro-Swell

The 4.0 'mix' model (the 3.9 'classic' one stays selectable): 1. every colour laid at 97% density — the paper and earlier strokes show through, and sub-steps never band · 2. crossings mix the two inks like an overprint (multiply), the same for every colour, white included · 3. the mix comes in fast as the nib meets the ink and fades slowly after it · 4. a 5–10% capillary swell at crossings, by ink (ruling pens 5%, Zig 7.5%, Posca 10%) · 5. arc-length gated self-crossing loops for continuous eye-gaze & boid trails.
