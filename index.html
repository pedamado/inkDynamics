About InkDynamics · SiX Research Project

SiX — Drawing for Social Re-connectivity

An interdisciplinary research project developing assistive, expressive digital drawing and calligraphic tools for individuals with motor impairments (ALS/ELA, locked-in syndrome) and creative communities.

Concept, Expressive Line & Dynamic Agents

Concept and design by Pedro Amado (Faculty of Fine Arts of the University of Porto / i2ADS Research Institute); development by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash in Antigravity and Anthropic Claude Opus 5.5 (October 2026).

Digital lines in drawing software often feel sterile, static, and disconnected from human gesture. When drawing through non-traditional interfaces—such as eye gaze dwell (in Inkwell and InkGaze) or micro hand gestures—the line itself must carry emotional vitality, visceral weight, and physical friction.

InkDynamics models the micro-physics of liquid fluid flow, capillary bristle depletion, pressurized mop waisting, and vibrating metallic sheet friction.

🖌️ Zig Chinese Calligraphy Brush (v2)

Capillary bristle split, velocity-based ink starvation, asymmetric "bone-head" touch-down flares, and paper tooth friction.

🟡 Posca Mop Squeezer (v3)

Monoline fluid acrylic core, dome touch-down stamps, speed waisting, border starvation stripes, and natural gravity-driven drip runs.

📐 Curved Ruling / Folded Cola Pen (v3.8)

Lance blade contour, angled pill ellipse touchdown, inverted friction skips (dense ladder vs wide skips), volcano pressure-release catenary decay, and directional provenance spit bursts.

📏 Flat Ruling / Chisel Pen (v3.8)

Extreme edge asymmetry (laser-straight spine vs jagged soundwave comb), dual corner train tracks, speed-flared teeth up to 3x nominal width, and directional spit bursts.

💧 Ink that mixes (v4.0)

Every colour laid at 97% density — the paper and earlier strokes show through — and mixed like an overprint where strokes cross: fast as the nib meets the ink, slowly fading after it, with a 5–10% capillary swell by ink.

Application Purpose

1. Integration into Inkwell: Powers the expressive stroke rendering engine in Inkwell 20 for eye-gaze and assistive drawing modes — every pen of the library, Inkwell's colours and papers, ink that mixes where strokes cross.

2. Standalone Library for Creative Coders: A zero-dependency, modular JavaScript library for any web developer, generative artist, or researcher wishing to add authentic, expressive physical line mechanics into their canvas applications.

3. Interactive 1-Line Snippet Generator: The toolbar provides live copy-pasteable constructor calls and configuration JSON objects to instantly bring your customized instruments into external apps.
