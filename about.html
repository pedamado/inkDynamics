InkDynamics

Physical Ink Mechanics, Expressive Calligraphic Lines & Assistive Art Making

SiX — Drawing for Social Re-connectivity

An interdisciplinary scientific research project exploring digital drawing, gaze interaction, and expressive lines as a vehicle for human connection, motor rehabilitation, and creative autonomy for individuals facing motor impairments and neurodegenerative conditions (such as Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis / ALS / ELA and locked-in syndrome).

1. The Concept & The Expressive Line

Digital drawing environments traditionally rely on sterile geometric abstractions: uniform circular brushes, smooth Bézier curves, or dead vector ribbons. While computationally clean, these abstractions strip away the very essence of human mark-making—the resistance, friction, fluid starvation, and organic micro-accidents that give a handwritten line its soul and expressive voice.

In assistive drawing systems such as Inkwell (which enables art-making via eye gaze dwell or minimal micro-movements), the expressiveness of the mark is paramount. When an artist cannot modulate a physical stylus with hand pressure, the software engine itself must imbue the line with kinematic intelligence and physical reality.

Conceived and designed by Pedro Amado (Faculty of Fine Arts of the University of Porto / i2ADS Research Institute), InkDynamics was created to simulate the real physics of liquid ink, deformable nibs, and fibrous paper substrates. It was developed by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash in Antigravity and Anthropic Claude Opus 5.5 (October 2026).

2. The Four Calibrated Physical Instruments

Each instrument in InkDynamics has been calibrated against high-resolution physical macro photographic specimens of real ink on textured paper:

🖌️ Zig Chinese Calligraphy Brush (v2)

Models capillary multi-bristle hair bundles. As stroke velocity accelerates, ink flow down the hairs is starved, exposing individual bristle tracks (hollow dry brush marks). Hesitation causes capillary pooling, while line touchdowns produce an asymmetric "tibia bone-head" contact flare.

🟡 Posca Mop Squeezer (v3)

Simulates a pressurized round sponge dome filled with dense, opaque acrylic paint. The core maintains 100% solid saturated ink, while the dome flanks experience longitudinal tooth starvation. Resting or dwelling triggers authentic gravity-driven downward drip cascades.

📐 Curved Ruling / Folded Cola Pen (v3.8)

Crafted from two tightly bound curved metal plates. Touchdown stamps an angled pill ellipse aligned with blade orientation. Inverted friction skips alternate between dense cross-bars and wide skips. Sudden pressure releases explode into volcano-like spit bursts that ease out in a fast catenary curve, accompanied by directional provenance spit droplets.

📏 Flat Ruling / Chisel Pen (v3.8)

Inspired by German Expressionist calligraphy and flat folded brass pens. Features severe edge asymmetry: one flank remains laser-sharp and clean, while the opposite trailing edge breaks into aggressive soundwave comb teeth (flaring up to 3x nominal stroke width), dual corner train tracks, and directional spit droplet showers.

💧 Ink That Mixes (v4.0)

Every colour is laid slightly transparent (97% density): the paper and the strokes beneath show through. Where strokes cross, the inks mix like an overprint — the mix comes in fast as the nib meets the ink and fades slowly after it — and the line swells 5–10%, by ink (least in the ruling pens' quick-drying ink, most in the Posca's heavy acrylic). Black stays black. Your own tunings are kept as presets (in the browser or on a server); the 13 core presets are permanent.

3. Dual Purpose & Open-Source Community Tool

InkDynamics fulfills two central roles:

  1. Core Engine for Inkwell 20: It provides the stroke rendering pipeline for the SiX project's gaze and assistive drawing applications (connecting with InkGaze.js, multi-agent boids with pens of their own, and accessibility inputs).
  2. Standalone Modular Library for Creative Coders: It is packaged as a standalone, zero-dependency JavaScript library (ESM and script tag) that any developer or artist can use to build custom drawing software, generative art pipelines, or expressive sketching tools.

4. Quickstart & Integration

Integrating InkDynamics into any HTML5 canvas takes a single line of code: 

import { createInkDynamics } from './InkDynamics/js/inkdynamics.js';

// 1-Line Instantiation
const ink = createInkDynamics({
  canvas: document.getElementById('myCanvas'),
  tool: 'ruling_pen_curved',
  preset: 'curved_ruling_medium',
  color: '#191817',
  thickness: 7.0,
  paperTooth: 0.45
});

// Drive with any coordinate stream (mouse, touch, eye gaze, or boids)
ink.penDown(x, y, 0.5);
ink.penMove(newX, newY, dt, 0.5);
ink.penUp(newX, newY);

You can also use the interactive 1-Line Call / Config JSON field in the lab toolbar to copy the exact initialization string for any pen after customizing its parameters.
